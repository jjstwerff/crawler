# Intent — what the Ortler render must read as (FROZEN)

Draw-skill step 1: frozen *before any mark*. **Do not edit these to match what renders** —
that goalpost drift is the deep self-deception trap. If reality proves a predicate wrong,
fix it with a dated note, don't silently relax it.

This is a **map**, so the live channel is **recognition**, not affect (feeling is out of
scope — same as the sprite rule in CLAUDE.md). The cold test: a viewer shown the render,
never told what it is, says *"an alpine mountain massif — glaciers up top, forested flanks,
a river valley below"* (ideally *"the Alps"*). If it reads as "rolling hills" or "uniform
terrain", it failed.

## Checkable gestalt predicates (judge at thirds/bands, not pixels)

- **P1 — glaciated core.** A bright snow/ice region sits on the **highest** ground (upper-
  middle of the grid), and is the **smallest** of the major zones.
- **P2 — vertical zonation order.** By elevation the bands read in order
  `valley < forest < alpine meadow/tundra < scree/bare rock < snow/ice` — concentric-ish
  around the high core, **not** random patches. Forest below ~2000 m, snow above ~3000 m.
- **P3 — valley corridor.** A continuous **low, flat** corridor (the Vinschgau / upper
  Adige) along one edge reads as **settled/agricultural** (fields/orchards), clearly lower
  than everything around it. It exits toward the **E/ESE** (Adige flows toward Meran).
- **P4 — rivers run the low ground.** Linear water threads follow valleys and **converge**
  toward the main corridor; **no water on ridgelines**; every thread visibly downhill.
- **P5 — mid bands dominate.** Forest + alpine together are the **largest** area; the snow
  core and the valley corridor are both minority regions.

## Recognition floor

Pass = a cold read names it an alpine massif with the three registers (glacier / forest /
valley) present and correctly ordered (P1–P3) and water sane (P4). Anything less is a gap to
route through the failure taxonomy in the README.
